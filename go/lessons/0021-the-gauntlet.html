The gauntlet: your graduation test

Quizzes measure recognition; interviews measure retrieval under pressure. This gauntlet simulates the real thing: timed questions in random order, graded by the only judge present — you, honestly. Answer aloud, standing if you can, and grade each answer the moment it ends: Solid (correct, fluent, no long gaps) · Shaky (got there, but stumbled) · Froze (timer beat you). No notes, no pausing.

~25 min · the finale — everything, under pressure

Graduation criteria

  • Round 1 and the Round-2 finale: solid, both times.
  • Round 2 overall: ≥80% solid, zero freezes.
  • Any miss links its lesson below the result — review, then rerun tomorrow. Spacing beats cramming; the second clean run is what makes it stick.

After graduation: take it to real humans

I'm your practice partner, but wisdom needs live fire:

  • r/golang — the busiest Go community; ask, lurk, see what practitioners argue about.
  • Gophers Slack — the official-ish Go community Slack; #newbies is for exactly you.
  • Go Forum — long-form questions answered by people who write Go for a living.

Or ask me to play interviewer anytime — I can raise difficulty, interrupt mid-answer like real interviewers do, and grade against these same criteria.
