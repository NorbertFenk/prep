Go quick reference

Channels

A channel is a typed pipe between goroutines: sends and receives synchronize by themselves.

The arrows

SyntaxMeaning
ch := make(chan string)Creates an unbuffered channel of strings.
ch <- vSend: v flows into ch; blocks until a receiver is ready.
v := <-chReceive: takes a value out; blocks until a sender offers one.
make(chan int, 4)Buffered: sends proceed until 4 values sit unread.
close(ch)No more sends; receivers then drain the buffer and get zero values.
v, ok := <-chok is false once the channel is closed and empty.
for v := range chReceives until the channel is closed.

Mnemonic: the arrow <- always points in the direction the value travels.

Blocking rules

Unbuffered send meets receive — a hand-off, both sides synchronize. Receive from a channel nobody sends to blocks forever; if no goroutine could ever send, the runtime panics with all goroutines are asleep - deadlock!. Sending on a closed channel panics.

Direction and choice

SyntaxMeaning
func f(ch chan<- string)Parameter may only send into the channel.
func f(ch <-chan string)Parameter may only receive from it.

Collecting a known number of results? Receive that many times — each receive proves one send. Producing until done? Close the channel and range over it.
