The gauntlet: your graduation test

Lesson 0013 · HTML · ~25 min · the finale — everything, under pressure

You've scored full marks on every quiz. Quizzes measure recognition; interviews measure retrieval under pressure. Today's gauntlet simulates the real thing: timed questions in random order, graded by the only judge present — you, honestly.

The rules

  1. Answer aloud, as if an interviewer asked. Recording yourself is strongly encouraged — listen back once.
  2. Grade each answer the moment it ends: Solid (correct, fluent, no long gaps) · Shaky (got there, but stumbled) · Froze (timer beat you).
  3. No notes, no pausing the timer. The timer turning red means wrap up your sentence.

Round 1 — the build test

Before the questions, one live task: open your editor and write, from memory, a complete valid page — skeleton, one heading, one paragraph with an abbreviation and a machine-readable date, one image with proper alt, one labelled text input with a submit button. Then validate it at validator.w3.org. Green banner or it counts as a Froze.

Graduation criteria

  • Round 1: a complete page and a green validator banner.
  • Round 2 overall: ≥80% solid, zero freezes.
  • Any miss links its lesson below the result — review, then rerun tomorrow. Spacing beats cramming; the second clean run is what makes it stick.
